01 Computer Vision · Deep Learning · Dataset

Handwritten Devanagari Conjunct Recognition

Collected and published HDCoCo, a dataset of 50,020 handwritten Devanagari conjunct consonant samples, a script combination largely absent from existing OCR datasets. Benchmarked recognition models with transfer learning. Published at the 17th IAPR International Workshop on Document Analysis Systems (DAS 2026); dataset on IEEE Dataport.
